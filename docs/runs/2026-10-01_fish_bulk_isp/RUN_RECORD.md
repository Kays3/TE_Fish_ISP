## TL;DR

`scripts/05_bulk_linear_isp.py` (CellOracle-style ridge network with
propagation) was run on the 162-library TEcount gene + TE matrix, with 30
configured TE regulators present in the fish annotation. **In both modes the
real regulators are indistinguishable from expression-matched random genes.**

Delete arm, 30 regulators vs 90 random pseudo-regulators (3 seeds × 30),
from `tables/05c_*_overall.tsv`:

| Mode | Median FDR<0.05 families, real vs random | Mann–Whitney p (hits) | Mann–Whitney p (median \|effect\|) | Regulators with empirical p ≤ 0.05 (expected 1.5) | Binomial excess p | Regulators passing BH |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Plain (within tissue × treatment) | 393 vs 371 | 0.70 | 0.74 | 1 (tasora, p_emp = 0.033) | 0.79 | 0 |
| Global TE axis removed (`remove_te_pcs: 1`) | 62.5 vs 68 | 0.17 | 0.95 | 0 (atrx, ythdc1: p_emp = 0.055) | 1.00 | 0 |

The overexpression arm gives the same picture. No regulator-specific TE
signal is demonstrated. (An earlier draft said "atrx/ythdc1 beat 95% of
random genes at 4.4%"; the proper empirical p, (1 + #random ≥ obs)/(1 + N),
is 0.055. See DC-10 in `docs/DESIGN_CHANGES.md`.)

## Findings

1. **A global TE-load axis dominates** (`tables/05b_*`, from
   `scripts/05b_te_axis_diagnostics.py`).
   - TE PC1 explains 29.3% of within-stratum TE variance (PC2: 3.5%).
   - 96.8% of families load with the same sign (binomial p < 1e-300).
   - It correlates with TE read fraction at r = 0.87 [0.83, 0.91]
     (p = 1e-48) and with gene PC1 at |r| = 0.83 [0.77, 0.87].
   - Its correlation with library QC is weaker: multimapping
     −0.24 (p = 0.002), unmapped +0.22 (p = 0.006), library size −0.16
     (p = 0.050). Fisher-z CIs, df = 151.
   - Regulators' |r| with the axis is not distinguishable from random
     genes: median 0.19 vs 0.16, Mann–Whitney p = 0.17.
   - The original ad-hoc diagnostic (`configs/diag_driver.py`) is kept for
     provenance only.
2. **Plain mode measures correlation with that axis.** 32,142 of 114,720
   tests (28%) reach FDR<0.05. Each regulator's predicted direction follows
   its correlation with TE PC1. Deleting tasora (+0.64) or safb (+0.62)
   "lowers" almost every TE family; deleting tp53 (−0.45) "raises" them.
   Expression-matched random genes predict TE families just as well (CV
   R²≥0.05 for 1,920–1,928 of 2,205 families, vs 1,912 for the real
   regulators) and produce as many hits (Mann–Whitney p = 0.70;
   `tables/05c_plain_*`).
3. **The result is robust to outliers, which does not make it valid.**
   Excluding the 8 source-study outliers gives:
   - effect Spearman ρ = 0.985 [0.9848, 0.9853] over 66,150 pairs;
   - significant-set overlap: Jaccard 0.80, hypergeometric p < 1e-300;
   - sign agreement among shared hits: 14,125/14,125 (exact 95% CI
     [0.9997, 1]).

   See `tables/outlier_sensitivity_delete.tsv`.
4. **Removing the axis does not reveal specific regulators.** After removing
   TE PC1: 992 families are predictable, 3,728 of 59,520 tests reach
   FDR<0.05 (6%), and real regulators look like random genes
   (Mann–Whitney p = 0.17; `tables/05c_rmTEpc1_*`). The two regulators
   closest to the edge, atrx and ythdc1, have empirical p = 0.055 and
   BH-FDR 0.82. atrx deletion predicts LINE families up (14/16 significant
   LINE hits), which agrees with ATRX's known role in repeat silencing, but
   it does not beat the control and is not reported.
5. **Over-correction caveat.** On synthetic data with planted edges, removing
   PC1 kept every true effect but added false positives, because real signal
   was part of the removed component. The random-gene controls were
   therefore run in this mode too.

## Interpretation

In this cohort, natural variation of TE-silencer mRNA levels across 162
libraries carries no detectable information about TE regulation beyond what
any gene of similar expression carries. That is unsurprising: these are
unperturbed animals, silencer transcript levels vary little, and
transcript level is a poor proxy for silencing activity. The bulk linear ISP
**cannot be used to nominate TE regulators in this dataset.** That
conclusion is supported because the random-gene control was run. Without
it, the plain run would have looked like 32k "significant" regulator → TE
effects.
