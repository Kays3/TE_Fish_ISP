## What exists elsewhere (private `TE_Ocean_Acidification` repository, reports of October 2026)

  `te-warming-aocellaris-20261003`, with a post-hoc re-analysis after removing mitochondrial rRNA,

  separated from a tank effect in those reports.

These are TE-expression analyses, not ISP, and their results are not restated here.

## What this folder is for

A place for any perturbation-model work on warming that is registered later, with the same rules as the fish ISP
run: matched random genes, positive controls, power by simulation, and a stated unit of replication. Because the
warming cohort has a single tank per temperature, a plan must say first which comparison can be made at all.
